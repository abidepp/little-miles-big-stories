Hi, I’m Ish. I write to hold on to the moments that make a journey: the first light over a valley, a little hand in mine, the quiet in between.

My partner has the same restless heart. So when our daughter came along, we didn’t stop exploring. We just learned to do it a little differently, with her right beside us.
